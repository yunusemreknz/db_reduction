## <u>Aim of the Study</u>
- To quantify the correlation between app-calculated risk scores and real-world SARS-CoV-2 transmission probabilities
- To assess how exposure factors, including contact duration, proximity, and infectiousness, influence the likelihood of infection
- To explore the effects of multiple or successive exposures on transmission risk
- record linkage
